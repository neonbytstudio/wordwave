Privacy Policy

WordWave — Vocabulary Learning App

Effective date: October 1, 2026

1Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how WordWave (“the App”), operated by NeonByte Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”), collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App on your Android device.

By downloading or using WordWave, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App and uninstall it.

Key point: WordWave does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location, and your vocabulary progress is stored on your device.

2Information We Collect

2.1 Information stored locally on your device

The following data is stored locally in the App’s private database and preferences. It stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App (or clear its data):

  • Learning progress — words learned, review history, quiz results, mastery levels, and weak-word stats.
  • Streaks & activity — daily activity records, streak counts, and streak-freeze status.
  • App preferences — theme (light/dark/system), daily reminder time, words-per-day, quiz size, and similar settings.
  • Favorites — words you mark as favorites.

2.2 Information collected automatically by third parties

To support analytics, stability, and advertising, the App integrates the following services, which may automatically collect certain technical information:

ServiceData that may be collectedPurpose
Google Firebase Analytics App interactions (e.g., words learned, quiz completed, pack unlocked), device model, OS version, country/region (approximate), app instance ID Understand feature usage and improve the App

Firebase Remote Config and Cloud Firestore are used only to deliver vocabulary content updates (word packs and definitions). These are content downloads — we do not upload your personal data to Firestore.

2.3 Information we do NOT collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, or account credentials (no sign-up required)
  • Contacts, call logs, SMS, photos, or media files
  • Precise location
  • Microphone, camera, or biometric data

3How We Use Information

  • To provide and operate the App’s core features (daily words, quizzes, reviews, streaks).
  • To schedule daily reminders — the reminder time you choose is used only on your device to schedule a local notification. We do not send notifications from a server and we do not use server time.
  • To save your progress so it persists between sessions.
  • To display advertising, including personalized ads where consent applies.
  • To perform analytics and aggregate statistics that help us improve the App (these do not identify you personally).
  • To detect, investigate, and fix crashes and technical problems.
  • To process purchases and restore your premium entitlements.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5Advertising

The App displays advertisements, including rewarded ads that you may choose to watch (for example, to earn a streak freeze). Ads are served by Google AdMob.

  • Personalized ads are shown only where permitted and, where required (for example in the EEA/UK), after you provide consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form shown on first launch.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads at any time in Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (wording may vary by device).
  • For more details, see Google’s Advertising Technologies and Privacy Policy.

6In-App Purchases

Purchases (such as premium or remove-ads options) are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your full payment card details — payments are handled entirely by Google. We only receive confirmation of whether a purchase or subscription is active, in order to unlock the corresponding features.

7Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services. Their collection and use of data is governed by their own privacy policies:

Firebase Analytics Firebase Crashlytics Firebase Remote Config Cloud Firestore Google AdMob Google Play Billing

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy Overview
  • Google Play & Data Safety

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

8Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local learning data is kept on your device until you uninstall the App or use the “clear data” option in Android settings — after which it is permanently deleted.
  • Analytics and crash data are retained by Google/Firebase according to their standard retention periods (typically up to 14 months for Analytics, 90 days for Crashlytics, unless configured otherwise).
  • Ad data is retained by Google per its own policies.
To delete your data: uninstall WordWave from your device. This removes the local database containing your learning progress, streaks, and preferences.

9Your Rights

Depending on your region (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent (for example, ad personalization consent) at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because WordWave stores your learning data only on your device and requires no account, most of this data is under your direct control — deleting the App deletes it. For any third-party (Google) data, you can use the tools provided by Google (for example Google Account – Data & privacy).

10Children’s Privacy

WordWave is a general-audience vocabulary learning app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a third-party service integrated into the App, please contact us and we will take prompt steps to delete it.

11Data Security

We protect information using reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) for network requests and the use of Google’s secured infrastructure for analytics and content delivery. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
